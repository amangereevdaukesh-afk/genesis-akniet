# Where the lake held light

Three late nineteenth-century Swiss photochrom prints from the Library of Congress. The collection lists no known restrictions on reproduction.

- [Lucerne and the Rigi](https://www.loc.gov/pictures/item/2001703122/)
- [Axenstrasse and Brunnen](https://www.loc.gov/pictures/item/2001703063/)
- [Urnersee and Pilatus](https://www.loc.gov/item/2001703110/)

The date and diary are fictional. The three-phrase instrumental score was composed for the museum demo.
